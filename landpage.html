Choose your island

More than 7,000 islands, four unmistakable moods. Start with these four — each one a different reason to book the flight.

Palawan

Limestone cliffs and hidden lagoons around El Nido, often called the most beautiful island group in the country.

Boracay

Powder-white sand and calm, warm water — built for slow mornings and long sunsets.

Cebu

Canyoneer through Kawasan Falls, then swim with whale sharks and sea turtles off the coast.

Siargao

The country's surfing capital — mangrove lagoons, island-hopping boats, and the break at Cloud 9.

Warm tables, warmer welcomes

Filipino hospitality is built around the table. Meals are communal, seconds are expected, and a stranger is usually just a guest you haven't fed yet — the spirit locals call bayanihan, or helping one another without being asked.

Every region cooks differently, but a few dishes travel the whole archipelago: smoky lechon roasted whole over charcoal, tangy adobo simmered in vinegar and soy, sour sinigang broth, and halo-halo stacked with ice, beans, and ube for dessert.

Time a visit around a fiesta and you'll find whole streets closed for food, music, and dancing — the clearest window into how the Philippines celebrates.
